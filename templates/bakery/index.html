Our Story

Sponge is Our Specialism.
Joy is Our Business.

At Twinklebell Confectionaries, we do one thing exceptionally well — the British sponge cake. Light, pillowy layers built on time-honoured technique, filled and finished with buttercreams and flavours that feel familiar yet never ordinary.

Founded in London by a true believer in the power of a proper bake, every cake that leaves our kitchen is made by hand, to order, with ingredients that taste of somewhere real. We ship across the UK to anyone who thinks a genuinely good cake is worth seeking out.
